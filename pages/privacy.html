Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Overview

XConvert Pro provides browser-based image tools at xconvert.pro. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit and use the service.

2. Images and files

Core image operations are designed to run locally in your browser. The core tools do not require an account, and ordinary processing is designed not to upload selected images to an XConvert Pro application server. Your browser, device, network provider, hosting infrastructure, or third-party resources may still process technical connection data.

You are responsible for ensuring that you are authorized to process any file you select.

3. Technical information

Web hosting and network infrastructure may process technical request data such as IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, referrer, timestamps, and security-related logs. Such processing is governed by the relevant infrastructure providers and applicable law.

4. Third-party resources

Pages may load CDNs, icon libraries, hosting resources, or other third-party services. When your browser connects to them, those providers may receive technical request information under their own policies.

5. Cookies and storage

XConvert Pro does not require first-party cookies for its core workflow. Browser storage may support preferences or PWA functionality. See our Cookie Policy.

6. Your choices

You can stop using the service, clear browser storage, or restrict third-party resources through browser controls. Some restrictions may affect functionality.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the service or legal requirements change. Privacy questions can be raised through the XConvert Pro GitHub repository. Do not publish confidential personal information in public issues.
