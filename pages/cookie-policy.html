Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage and other browser-side storage.

2. XConvert Pro

The core tools do not require an account or first-party tracking cookie. Browser storage may be used for functional preferences or PWA behavior.

3. Third parties

CDNs and other external resources may receive technical request data and may use their own technologies under their policies.

4. Future analytics or advertising

If analytics, advertising, or other non-essential technologies are introduced, this policy will be updated and applicable consent controls will be used where required.

5. Consent controls

When you first visit, XConvert Pro shows a consent panel where you can accept all optional categories, allow only necessary storage, or customize your choices. Your selection is stored locally under a consent key so the banner does not reappear on every page.

You can reopen Cookie settings from the footer at any time. Necessary storage remains enabled because it supports core functions such as remembering consent and interface preferences.

6. Optional categories

Preferences may remember optional interface choices. Analytics and Marketing are disabled by default and are intended for future optional technologies; enabling a category does not itself load a service that is not installed.

7. Browser controls

You can also manage or delete cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. Blocking resources may affect some features.
