1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small data files stored by a website in a browser. Similar technologies include local storage and other browser-side storage mechanisms.

2. Necessary storage

XConvert Pro may use local browser storage for functions such as remembering consent choices, interface preferences or PWA behavior. These functions are intended to support the service rather than advertising.

3. Optional categories

Preferences may remember optional interface choices. Analytics and Marketing are disabled by default and are intended for optional technologies if they are introduced. Enabling a category does not itself install a service that is not present.

4. Third parties

CDNs, hosting providers, social services and other external resources can receive technical request data when your browser connects to them. Their own policies govern their processing.

5. Consent

Where consent is required by applicable law for optional cookies or similar technologies, the site should request consent before activating those technologies. You can reopen Cookie settings from the footer and manage browser storage through your browser controls.

6. Changes

This policy may change when the site's technologies or legal requirements change. The latest revision date appears above.
