Contact Us

Use the XConvert Pro GitHub repository for questions, bug reports, accessibility feedback, privacy requests and legal notices.

How to contact us

Open the project's public repository and choose the appropriate issue or discussion channel. For privacy or legal requests, clearly state the type of request without publishing unnecessary personal or confidential information.

Privacy and data requests

If your message concerns access, correction, deletion, objection, consent withdrawal or another data-protection right, identify the request clearly. We may need reasonable information to understand and process the request, subject to applicable law.

Security reports

Do not publish credentials, access tokens, private keys, payment-card data or other sensitive information in a public issue. If a report contains sensitive security details, use a private reporting channel provided by the repository when available.
